# Progression and Adventure Mode

VEPA utilizes a progression system to regulate cognitive load and provide a sense of mastery, transforming the simulation from a "complex sandbox" into a "guided discovery engine."

## Mode Structure
* **Adventure Mode:** Guided progression with mechanic unlocking. Players earn permission to manipulate deeper cosmological laws.
* **Architect Mode:** Unrestricted sandbox with full simulation access and no safety constraints.

## Core Progression Loop
Observe → Experiment → Discover → Complete Missions → Gain XP → Unlock Mechanics → Cause Bigger Problems → Learn New Systems → Repeat

## Unlock Structure
Concepts are unlocked in conceptual clusters rather than individual sliders:
1. **Basic Force Controls**
2. **Species Differentiation**
3. **Reproduction & Mutation**
4. **Signal Channels & Conductivity**
5. **Tidal Forces & Ecosystem Analytics**
6. **Void Core Events & Neural Swarm Detection**
7. **Architect Mode**

## XP and Mission Systems
XP is tied to experimentation, discovery, and ecosystem management rather than grinding.
* **Discovery XP:** Finding new phenomena (e.g., Lattice Lock).
* **Stability XP:** Maintaining biodiversity or equilibrium.
* **Chaos XP:** Documenting catastrophic outcomes (e.g., Mass Extinction).
* **Codex XP:** Researching systems through the Codex.

### Missions
* **Scientific:** Stable orbits, low entropy states.
* **Ecological:** Symbiotic species, biodiversity maintenance.
* **Chaos:** Tidal collapses, signal cascades.
* **Discovery:** Observing emergent synchronization.
